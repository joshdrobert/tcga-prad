Primary Research Question

Can open GDC clinical XML supplements and bulk RNA sequencing in TCGA-PRAD support reliable, transportable prediction of prostate cancer recurrence, and how do endpoint ambiguity, predictor chronology, and missingness affect benchmark validity?

Key Scientific Findings

1. Primary Paired Molecular Benchmark (n=187): On identical participants, pure baseline clinical staging achieved an AUROC of 0.710 (95% CI: 0.621–0.791), Brier score of 0.1673 (95% CI: 0.1366–0.2018), and calibration slope of 0.756, whereas adding the 13-gene expression panel yielded 0.703 (95% CI: 0.605–0.792), Brier score of 0.1672 (95% CI: 0.1329–0.2057), and calibration slope of 0.511 (paired ΔAUROC: -0.008, 95% CI: [-0.073, +0.056]; paired ΔBrier: -0.0001, 95% CI: [-0.0198, +0.0199]). The molecular panel provided no statistically significant incremental discrimination or Brier score improvement, while compressing the calibration slope from 0.756 to 0.511.

2. Predictor Chronology & Landmark Setting: Follow-up is indexed from initial diagnostic biopsy (Day 0 for 97.1% of patients [305/314; 98.1% of 311 patients with non-missing diagnostic method]), while surgical pathology and RNA sequencing are landmarked post-radical prostatectomy with an unmeasured biopsy-to-surgery interval. The XML psa_value was measured at a median of 511.0 days post-initial diagnosis (271 of 276 values post-baseline), representing post-treatment surveillance monitoring rather than pre-operative baseline PSA, and was strictly excluded from baseline Cox survival models.

3. Endpoint Concordance & Second Primary Separation: Biochemical recurrence agreed 100.0% (269/269) with cBioPortal, while disease-free survival agreed in 92.3% (239/259), uncovering 20 patients with XML-documented recurrence labeled DiseaseFree in cBioPortal. Two patients with non-prostatic new primary tumors (TCGA-CH-5791, TCGA-HC-7080) were adjudicated and separated from prostate recurrence.

4. Survival Analysis from Initial Diagnosis: In continuous-time survival analysis from initial diagnosis, median recurrence-free survival was not reached in any cohort. Strict primary sensitivity analysis withholding 7 unverified contradictory BCR dates (n=248, 57 dated events, 191 double-negatives) showed an out-of-fold Cox concordance of 0.672 (apparent 0.701; 1-yr RFS: 88.1%, 5-yr RFS: 65.3%), compared with 0.670 when admitting 46 asymmetric records (n=294). Permissive sensitivity benchmarking (admitting 7 unverified dates; n=255 and n=301, 64 dated events) achieved out-of-fold concordance of 0.649 and 0.646. Time origin is explicitly indexed to initial pathologic diagnosis.

When evaluated strictly on biochemical recurrence, local XML curation matches cBioPortal with 100.0% concordance (269/269). The 20 discrepancies in Disease-Free Survival represent patients with XML follow-up new tumor events or secondary progressions not indexed as disease events in cBioPortal's primary DFS attribute.

Discordance Root-Cause Analysis

  • Biochemical Recurrence Harmonization: Both sources extract identical BCR indicators when XML follow-up forms explicitly document PSA elevation (>0.2 ng/mL).
  • New Tumor Events (NTE): cBioPortal DFS status did not consistently ingest secondary locoregional recurrences, distant metastases, or post-hormone therapy progressions documented in later clinical XML updates.
  • Second Primary Malignancies: Two patients (TCGA-CH-5791, TCGA-HC-7080) developed second non-prostatic malignancies; our clinical audit separates these from prostate recurrence.

Historical Benchmark Context (n=314 / n=228)

For historical reproducibility, earlier iterations evaluated models on unadjudicated cohorts where uncertain records were naively mapped to zero:

  • Historical Full Cohort (n=314): Clinical AUROC 0.689 vs Imputed RNA AUROC 0.681. Imputation degraded calibration slope from 0.777 to 0.547.
  • Historical Paired (n=228): Clinical AUROC 0.689 vs RNA AUROC 0.686 (ΔAUROC -0.003, p=0.954).
  • Conclusion: Whether evaluated on historical unadjudicated (n=228) or definitive adjudicated (n=187) sets, the transcriptomic panel provides zero statistically significant incremental benefit.

Selection Bias Analysis (20 Excluded Patients)

Thirteen undated prostate recurrence events, two second non-prostatic primary cancers, and five fully indeterminate cases were excluded from strict exact-time survival analysis. These 20 patients exhibited 90.0% T3/T4 stage (vs 60.9% in included) and 65.0% documented recurrence (13/20). Excluding undated events slightly enriches the survival cohort with lower-risk cases, making the reported RFS conservative upper bounds.

Research Integrity & Limitations

Formal documentation of study limitations and Osaka 2027 conference decision gate.

Osaka Theranostics Congress Decision Gate: DEFINITIVE NO-GO

Rationale: The 9th Theranostics World Congress requires research addressing radioligand therapies, PSMA-PET/CT molecular imaging, or targeted radionuclide dosimetry. TCGA-PRAD is a surgical radical prostatectomy cohort with 0% theranostics content. Submitting genomic recurrence prediction to a radiopharmaceutical meeting would violate scientific integrity. The project is repurposed as an oncology informatics paper for AUA / AMIA 2027.

Key Study Limitations

  • Multi-Center Retrospective Staging: TCGA tissue samples were acquired from participating medical centers without centralized pathology re-review.
  • Molecular Missingness: 74 of 261 definitive patients (28.4%) lacked RNA sequencing; models should not be evaluated by naive imputation.
  • Surveillance PSA Measurement Timing: Follow-up PSA was measured at a median of 511 days post-diagnosis, limiting its utility as a true pre-operative baseline biomarker.
  • External Generalization: All performance estimates are 5-fold cross-validated internal estimates; independent multi-institutional validation is required before clinical adoption.
