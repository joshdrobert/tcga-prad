# TCGA-PRAD CONFERENCE & JOURNAL VENUE FIT ANALYSIS

## Why TCGA-PRAD is a NO-GO for Osaka 2027 (9th Theranostics World Congress)
1. **Zero Theranostic Content:** TCGA-PRAD is a surgical radical prostatectomy cohort with bulk RNA-seq and follow-up serum PSA. It contains zero PSMA-PET/CT imaging, zero radioligand therapies ($^{177}\text{Lu}$-PSMA-617), zero radiation dosimetry, and zero theranostic target binding measurements.
2. **Scientific Misrepresentation Risk:** Rebranding post-surgical recurrence modeling as "theranostics" would be scientifically indefensible and subject to summary rejection by the congress scientific committee.

## Optimal Oncology & Clinical Informatics Venues
1. **American Urological Association (AUA) Annual Meeting 2027:**
   - Track: Prostate Cancer: Basic Science & Clinical Biomarkers
   - Direct fit: Evaluates the real clinical utility of molecular panels against standard D'Amico / NCCN clinical staging post-prostatectomy.
2. **AMIA Clinical Informatics Conference 2027 / AMIA Annual Symposium:**
   - Track: Data Harmonization, Clinical Data Registries, and Machine Learning Evaluation
   - Direct fit: Resolves discrepancies between primary GDC clinical XML files and secondary curated portals (cBioPortal), proving the dangers of naive median imputation in missing multi-omic data.
3. **Journal Targets:**
   - *JCO Clinical Cancer Informatics* (ASCO)
   - *Journal of Urology*
   - *Scientific Data* (Nature Portfolio)
